What are you here to do?

Try it

One file with a model inside. Download, run, ask. Nothing to install.

./goinfer-chat-0.5b-<os>-<arch>

Embed it

A Go library. Ask a question, get a filled-in struct back.

go get github.com/
  townsendmerino/goinfer

Connect your tools

OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible. Setup notes for Claude Code, opencode and jevx.

goinfer-serve -web -model
  ~/models/model.gguf

Run bigger models

Ask whether a model fits your machine before loading it, and how.

goinfer-chat fit <model>
