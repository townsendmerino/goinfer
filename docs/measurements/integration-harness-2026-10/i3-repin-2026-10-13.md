# I3: the serve binary re-pinned before the run (2026-10-13)

An amendment to "I3 run plan, registered 2026-10-13, before the run" in `docs/tasks/task-integration-harness-2026-10.md`.
Made on the owner's word ("fix and re-pin"), after the plan was registered and before any job of the night queue ran.
It changes the server under the Codex cells. It does not change the pass rules, the VOID rule, the predictions, the
cells, or the queue. The swap of the 7B for Qwen3-4B, made by another session the same afternoon, is not described here.

## What was wrong

The pilot lead "Codex's read answer on Mellum began `5</think> maybe? Or just "5"?`" had two causes.

1. **Codex sends no `max_output_tokens`, and `/v1/responses` gave such a request 512 tokens**, the default of the other
   routes, thinking included. `connect` had written Codex's configuration with an output limit of a quarter of the
   window. In the pilots' wire logs on nobara-pc (five pilot directories of the harness session and two of the KV0
   traffic study) none of 82 Codex requests carried a cap, and 4 ended at exactly 512 output tokens: 2 of 15 in the
   Mellum pilot that produced the lead and 2 of 11 in one 7B pilot.
2. **The reasoning budget is three quarters of the turn**, so it closed Mellum's think block at 384 tokens. Mellum
   went on reasoning in the answer and wrote its own `</think>`, which the splitter passed through as text. Under
   Mellum's tokenizer the reply is 1 open tag + 384 reasoning tokens + 1 forced close + 126 answer tokens = 512.

## What changed

| commit | change |
|---|---|
| `27378a4b` | `chat/reasoning.go:ThinkSplitter.Push` drops a close delimiter that arrives in the answer (a partial one at the tail is held). |
| `81791568` | A `/v1/responses` request that names no cap gets `internal/connect/connect.go:OutputLimit` of the window: a quarter of it, at least 1,024 and at most 8,192. The other routes keep 512. |

Both are on `main`, each with a test that fails without it (`TestThinkSplitter_table`, the stray-close case of
`TestStreamTokens_thinkSplit`, `TestResponses_noCapTakesTheWindowsOutputLimit`).

## What it changes in the plan

Only the Codex cells: opencode and Claude Code send their own cap (the one `connect` wrote), so their requests are
served as before.

| window | Codex output cap, first pin | re-pinned | reasoning budget of a Codex turn, first pin | re-pinned |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16,384 | 512 | 4,096 | 384 | 3,072 |
| 9,408 (the I1 live gate) | 512 | 2,352 | 384 | 1,764 |

- **The I1 live gate's rule is untouched.** A prompt is refused only when it reaches the window, as before. The room
  counted for a reply in the "compaction cannot help" reading goes from 1,024 to 2,352 tokens at 9,408; Codex's fixed
  part is under its 5,850-token first request, and 5,850 + 2,352 = 8,202 is under 9,408, so an overflow there still
  reads as curable.
- **The predictions were written from pilots under the 512 cap** and are left as written. A cell that a 512-token cut
  would have failed can now pass; read the Codex cells with that in mind.
- **Wall estimates are not re-made.** A Codex turn can now run to 4,096 tokens where it stopped at 512. The per-run
  timeouts and each job's deadline are unchanged and bound it.
- **Grading:** the wire log records `output_tokens` for every request, so a turn that ended at the cap can still be
  counted per run (4,096 or 2,352 now, where it was 512).
