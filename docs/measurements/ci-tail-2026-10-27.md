## 2. The change

The tail's `-skip` adds `^TestDecisionCensus$|^TestDecisionCensus_secondProcess$`. `test-decoder-a` runs
`^TestDecisionCensus$` on `./decoder/` in a new step after its sampling step, and `test-decoder-d` runs
`^TestDecisionCensus_secondProcess$`, both on the same `go test -race -timeout 25m -tags goinfer_testhooks -json` line
as every other shard, with a zero-match guard and a summary step. No job is added or renamed, so the 19 required checks
are unchanged and branch protection needs no edit.

Both keep -race: the census is not pure. `buildCensus` runs its D1, D2 and load phases through `parallelEach`, which
starts `runtime.NumCPU()` goroutines, and the census's mutex guards their writes.

Expected on the slow class: the tail about 1,169 − 658 ≈ 510 s of test step, about 9.3 minutes of job; shard a about
72 + 364 s plus the decoder compile, about 8 minutes; shard d about 140 + 295 s, about 8 minutes. Moving
`_secondProcess` out of the same process as `TestDecisionCensus` loses the shared `censusLoaded` cache, so its own
in-process census now loads the fixtures itself; the first run says what that costs.
