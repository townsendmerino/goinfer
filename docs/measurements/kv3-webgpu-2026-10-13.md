# KV3, WebGPU's half: admission by the pass's own scratch figure (2026-10-13)

`docs/tasks/task-kv-architecture-2026-10.md` §6 KV3; the CUDA half is `docs/measurements/kv3-cuda-2026-10-13.md` §3. Machine: nobara-pc,
WebGPU on Vulkan (RTX 2070 SUPER). Branch `kv3-webgpu-2026-10`, from `kv3-cuda-2026-10`. The Metal half is the KV-pre session's.

## 1. Facts and the change (read, before any code)

- `gpu/residency.go:residentDecoder.PrefillLast` already prefills in passes of `prefillBlockRows` (512) rows and every pass allocates its
  scratch afresh (`prefillLastRows`); WebGPU's per-row kernels have no kernel class by length (KV0: every invariance arm bit-identical), so the
  tail minimum stays the default 8.
- It reports nothing to admission, so a WebGPU resident request is priced at the whole-prompt CPU-sweep figure (`prefillScratchBytes`).
- **Measured before registering (a throwaway counter, not committed):** the pass's fresh device buffers are linear in the rows: 14,103
  B/row on the tiny fixture (7.2 MB at 512 rows), 113,090 B/row on the 1.5B (55.2 MiB at 512 rows). The layer-to-layer recycling (R-25)
  keeps the pass at about one layer's buffers, not the model's. An analytic sum of one layer's buffers with no recycling is 4% above the
  measurement on the 1.5B (29.5k floats a row against 28.3k).
- **The change:** `residentDecoder` implements `decoder.ResidentPrefillChunks`: `PrefillChunkRows` is `prefillBlockRows`, and
  `PrefillScratchBytes(rows)` is the analytic one-layer sum, rows × 4 bytes × (the activation quantisations, q, k, v, the attention
  context and its quantisation, the o-projection output, gate and up, the gated quantisation, the down output, and the residual stream),
  plus the fixed part (the head's vocab-sized staging buffer, the last row, and 256 bytes for each of the pass's small uniform buffers).

## 2. Gates, registered before code

- **Price: never under the pass, and close.** For rows in {16, 64, 300, 512}, on the tiny fixture and the real 1.5B at int4, `PrefillScratchBytes(rows)`
  is **at least the bytes the pass creates** (measured by a test seam in `prefillLastRows`, as CUDA's `prefillScratchSeenForTest`) and **at most 1.25
  times** it. A figure under the pass fails: it is what admission trusts. (CUDA's was exact because its buffers are not recycled; the 25% allows
  the recycling the analytic sum ignores.)
- **A request the old bound refused now answers.** On the real 1.5B with WebGPU, with the host's available memory injected between the
  whole-prompt price and the per-chunk price of a 20,954-token request, admission accepts it, and refuses it when the room is under the chunk's
  price; the pass on a 12,500-token prompt then runs (the KV0 arms already show 20k-token passes: their per-chunk bytes are the same).
- **Identity: nothing about execution changes.** The WebGPU prefill suites and the KV0 WebGPU arms (`TestKV0_prefillInvariance_*`) pass unchanged;
  the change is pricing only.
- **Estimates:** every gate here is a day check (seconds to a few minutes).

## 3. Readings (2026-10-13)

Files: `kv3-webgpu-2026-10-13/gpu-identity-day.txt`. Tests: `gpu/kv3_scratch_test.go`; the code is `gpu/prefill_scratch.go` and a size-reporting seam in
`gpu/prefillrunner.go` (`prefillBufSeenForTest`, nil in production).

- **Price: never under, within 1.06 over.** The figure divided by the bytes the pass creates, tiny fixture at 16, 64, 300, 512 rows: 1.056, 1.052,
  1.050, 1.050; real 1.5B: 1.040, 1.047, 1.049, 1.050 (registered: at least 1.0 and at most 1.25). The first formula, written from the measurement above,
  failed both ways in the first run (25.5% to 28.7% over on the tiny fixture, 8% under at 16 rows on the 1.5B): it priced the sandwich norms
  for every family, the uniform buffers at 256 bytes (they are 16 to 64) and one head staging buffer (the pass creates two). It was corrected
  against the pass's own buffer sizes and re-run; the bar was not moved. Not measured: a family with its own per-layer geometry or sandwich norms
  (Gemma); they are priced at the widest layer and with the sandwich terms, untested.
- **A request the old bound refused now answers.** Real 1.5B under WebGPU, a 20,954-token request: whole-prompt price **1.65 GB**, per-chunk price **0.180 GB**;
  admitted with the room between them, refused under the chunk's price.
- **Identity.** `-run 'Prefill|KV0|Batched'`, heavy tier: 36 passes, 7 skips (fixtures or checkpoints this worktree does not have), and 1 failure,
  `TestPrefillMRoPE_realImage` ("the image prompt's device prefill sits further from the CPU's than the text control allows: 0.9396 under
  0.9280"). **It fails identically, to the digit, on the unchanged `kv3-cuda-2026-10` base**, so it is not this change; it is a Qwen2.5-VL image fidelity
  bar on WebGPU/Vulkan that is red on this tree already. The KV0 WebGPU arms and every `TestPrefill*` that ran pass.
