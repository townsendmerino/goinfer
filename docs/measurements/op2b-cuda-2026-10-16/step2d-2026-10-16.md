# OP2b on CUDA, step 2d: `ResidentLayerOps` is the only source of a layer's kind (2026-10-16)

Registered in `docs/tasks/task-forward-architecture-2026-10.md` §4.7, "OP2b on CUDA" (step 2 is four PRs). nobara-pc, RTX 2070 SUPER, driver 595.91.07, GPU runs under the timing lock. Base `a5ed08fa` (main with 2c), change `2729ac44`. **No new behaviour, nothing declared.**

## What changed

- The agreement check step 2a added (a layer where `ResidentLayerOps` and `Qwen35LinearLayer` or the MLA geometry disagree was declined by name) is gone, and `Qwen35LinearLayer` is no longer read anywhere in `cuda/`.
- `hlayer.isDeltaNet`, `hlayer.isMLA`, `cudaLayer.isDeltaNet` and `cudaLayer.isMLA` are not stored: the layer ones are methods that read `mixer` (`mamba_resident.go`), and the build's reads compare `hl.mixer`. A layer's kind has one source.
- Kept on purpose: `dnetOK`, `mlaOK` and `dnAttnGate` as model-level geometry (the parameters of the DeltaNet and MLA kernels, the per-32 activation and softcap declines, `qGate` for the gated softmax layer, a family variant and not a mixer kind), and `cudaResident.isMLA` (a model-wide flag).
- `TestLayerOps_agreeWithTheFamilyPredicates` becomes `TestLayerOps_buildTheKindsTheFamilyHas`: it no longer compares two copies of the same field, it checks each built layer's `(mixer, ffn)` against the family's own layer predicate or block kind, on `llama-tiny`, `deepseek-tiny`, `qwen35-tiny`, `olmo_hybrid-tiny` and now `nemotron-tiny`.
