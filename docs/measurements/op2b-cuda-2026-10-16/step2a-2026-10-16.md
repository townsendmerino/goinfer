## What changed

`BuildResident` takes each layer's mixer and feed-forward flag from `decoder.Model.ResidentLayerOps`; the layer-kind switch and `launchToken`'s DeltaNet and MLA
branches dispatch on them. **Transitional:** the old predicates (`Qwen35LinearLayer`, `mlaOK`) are still computed, a layer where they disagree with
`ResidentLayerOps` is declined by name, and `isDeltaNet` and `isMLA` stay as derived booleans for the sites that read them. All three go in step 2d. The Mamba
and none mixers are declined by name until 2b.
