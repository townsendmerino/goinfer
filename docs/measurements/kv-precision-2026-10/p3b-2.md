**The build (`metal/kv_i8_prefill.go`, `metal/prefill.go`).** At `--kv i8` the f16 MMA pass now runs. Per attention layer:
1. It stores its block's K/V into the int8 cache with `kv_store_i8_prefill`: `kv_store_i8`'s source, with each K/V read widened
   from the pass's half values.
2. It dequantizes rows `[0, startPos+M)` of that layer's cache, as `half(code × scale)`, into a per-call f16 scratch
   (`kv_dequant_i8_f16`).
3. It runs the existing attention kernels on the scratch.

No f16 kernel is edited; the two kernels are appended to the prefill library. The scratch, `4 × padded context × kv_dim` bytes,
is priced in `prefillScratchBytes`, so MEM7's block sizer counts it. The int8 declines in `prefillOK`, the kernel floor,
`PrefillPath`, the pass, `PrefillBlockRows` and the step routing are lifted; f32 keeps every one. Prompts below the step's
ceiling still take the step at int8, bit-identical to sequential.

## G2's failure, diagnosed

The registered G2 measures how far the int8 pass is from the int8 step, against how far f16's pass is from f16's step. The two
int8 arms store different int8 caches: the pass computes K/V with its own GEMMs, and a K/V difference far below f16's rounding
moves a value across an int8 rounding boundary, a step of amax/127 per element. That step is far coarser than f16's rounding, so
the two int8 caches differ by more than the two f16 caches do, and the arms drift apart even though each is a valid int8 cache.
The diagnosis grades both int8 arms against one reference, the shipped f16 sequential decode. There the int8 pass is not worse
than the int8 step; it is nearer the reference in every cell (KL ratio 0.735, UCB 0.897; agreement +1.23 pts; 19 hard flips
against 33). That fits the pass computing its attention from f16 activations as its K/V's neighbourhood does, but the record
claims only the measurement.

**Amended after the data, on the owner's word (D10 (a), owner, 2026-10-22).** P3B2-G2 is now the diagnosis's form: every arm
scored against the f16 sequential decode, the int8 pass non-inferior to the int8 step, by the same criteria. Side by side:
