The build is otherwise unchanged: the Metal resident's `kvF32` and `kvI8` name the GQA K/V cache and do not apply to an MLA layer,
whose latent was f32 already, so MLA's paths are what they were. The MLA int8 decline in the build is removed, because the resolver
now refuses that request before a build sees it. The `--kv` help text names MLA on Metal as f32.

**The fit guard's price is unchanged.** `kvPricingFor` prices a Metal load at f32 where the resolver delivers f32 (P3a). For an MLA
model that answer would double the price, because `kvBytesForCtx` counts K plus V and Metal holds one f32 latent per layer, which
is exactly the K-plus-V price at 2 B. The MLA exception keeps it at what is allocated
(`TestKVPricingFor_metalMLAPricedAsAllocated`: 294,912 B for 1,024 positions on the deepseek_v2 config). A plant without the
exception turns it red: 589,824 B priced. The guard admits what it admitted before.
