**Correction (2026-10-27, while running G-NP-4).** Three of the 8 counted rows were started by the queue by day, not
at night: row 3 (10-14 at 11:18), row 4 (10-15 at 13:10) and row 5 (10-17 at 19:23). Their readings describe the day.
The finding below holds for the rows that started between 22:00 and 06:30. Those are rows 1, 2, 6, 7 and 8 and the
uncounted `s6-e4b`; they printed 2.9–7.5 GB available (median 6.65 GB), against the profile's 12.2.

**What differs is the memory available.**
- The real refusals printed 2.9–7.7 GB available (median 6.6 GB across the 11 readings, `s6-e4b` included).
- The night profile assumes 12.2 GB. The Mac's nights in this window were not idle: apps were left open.
- The day profile (7.0 GB) refuses 7 of the 8. The 8th, row 8, fits even there: it was refused at 2.9 GB, well below the
  apps-open reading.

So this is a finding about which profile prices the Mac's night jobs (Francis's 1b, "the idle profile prices night jobs"),
as the brief anticipated, not a defect in `fit -profile` or `night.py add`.
