## 1. What it is

`python3 scripts/night.py wait NAME [--timeout MIN]` finds the job queued or running under that name and follows that
instance, by its `seq`, through its re-queues (a check's refusal, a stop) until its final record. It prints one line,
the outcome and the log path, and exits:
- 0 for `ok`;
- 1 for any other final outcome (FAILED, timeout, VOID-refused);
- 2 for no such job, or one dropped while waited on;
- 124 on its own `--timeout`.

A job already finished when `wait` starts returns its newest record at once. It polls local files every
`NIGHT_WAIT_POLL_S` (15 s). From the other box, one background
`ssh <box> 'cd <repo> && python3 scripts/night.py wait NAME'` is one completion notification, in place of a session
sleeping and polling over ssh. Nothing else in `night.py` changes.

Three mutations (`gate mutation`, [`w2-mutations-2026-10-26.log`](pw4-2026-10/w2-mutations-2026-10-26.log)) turn the
tests red:
- a retry record taken as final;
- following records instead of the queued instance;
- every outcome exiting 0.

**Two found on the way:**
- **The retry-record mutation first stayed green.** `finish()` re-queues a refused job before it unlinks the running
  file, so `wait` never sees the job gone with a retry record. The guard stays, since a reorder of `finish()` would
  otherwise make `wait` report a refusal as the job's end, and a test now opens that window by hand.
- **The instance mutation first stayed green.** The live check matters only for a job dropped while waited on, which no
  test covered; one does now.
