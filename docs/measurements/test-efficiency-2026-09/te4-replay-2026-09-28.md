## 1. The rule, fixed before any replay (TE4-SEQ-v1)

Written into this file at 2026-09-28 10:46 PDT (17:46 UTC), before `te4_replay.py` existed. Disclosure of what had been
seen by then: the recorded verdicts and summary ratios in the task docs (they are the thing being reproduced), the
JSON field layout of `gate5-served.json`, and the per-pair lines of the three in-process logs while checking their
format. No replay, interval or boundary had been computed on any gate.

### 1.1 Unit and pairing

- **Served gates (`bench_peer.py`).** A cell is one engine × model × depth × config: restart the server, one discarded
  warm-up completion, then `runs` × `NCOMP` timed completions, then teardown. `counts.completion_rates[]` keeps every
  timed completion (a decode window of `NGEN` tokens at a fixed depth), and `runs[r]` is the mean of run *r*'s
  completions. **The smallest pairable unit is one completion.** The two arms' cells run one after the other (for a
  three-engine plan: arm A's cell, the peer's cell, arm B's cell), so no two completions are simultaneous. A pair is
  completion *k* of run *r* in the new arm's cell with completion *k* of run *r* in the old arm's cell, same pass, same
  model / depth / config: the same prompt, at the same position after its cell's warm-up. Pairs never cross passes.
  The paired observation is d = ln(new rate) − ln(old rate).
- **Block (the look granularity) = one run of each arm** (`NCOMP` pairs). A look is taken after every block. Two-pass
  gates are replayed pass 1's blocks, then pass 2's, in recorded order.
- **In-process ABBA gates** (R-06 gate 3, S0 and S0b gate 2, named as seeds): the unit and the block are one logged
  ABBA pair, d = ln(the logged "ON is N× faster").
- **Looks are taken only where n ≥ 3 paired observations** (served: every block; in-process: pairs 3, 4, 5).

### 1.2 The interval

At look *j*: n_j paired observations, mean d̄_j, sample sd s_j, SE_j = s_j / √n_j.

- **Boundaries:** Lan–DeMets O'Brien–Fleming-type spending, α*(t) = 2 − 2Φ(z₀.₉₇₅ / √t), two-sided α = 0.05 (no gate
  registered another α). t_j = n_j / n_cap, the information fraction by pairs, where n_cap is the pairs at the recorded
  N. The two-sided symmetric z boundaries c_j for the looks actually taken are solved by recursive numerical
  integration (Armitage–McPherson–Rowe) on the Brownian-motion representation.
- **Unknown variance:** each c_j is carried to the t scale at the same nominal tail, c*_j = T⁻¹_{n_j − 1}(Φ(c_j)).
- **Bias allowance δ** for what within-cell variance cannot see. Each served arm is a single cell per pass, so a
  cell-level effect (server start, layout, the box's state during that cell) is confounded with the treatment and no
  within-gate estimate covers it; order effects likewise (arm A always runs first within a pass).
  - served: **δ = 0.022** (log units, ≈ 2.2%). Source: the owner's 2026-09-28 09:05 amendment to the L1 arm64 fix —
    "the largest pass-to-pass difference the two order-reversed passes measured on this Mac earlier today". It is the
    only measured order/cell allowance on record today; TE3's noise registry replaces it per machine × instrument.
  - in-process ABBA: **δ = 0**. Both arms share one loaded process and alternate inside every pair, so linear drift
    cancels within the pair and pair-to-pair spread is already in SE.
- **Repeated confidence interval:** I_j = [d̄_j − c*_j·SE_j − δ, d̄_j + c*_j·SE_j + δ].

### 1.3 Stopping and the verdict

Per graded model (a sub-gate), with the gate's ship bar S and kill bar K on the new ÷ old ratio (K = S where a gate has
a single bar, e.g. "SHIP if ≥ 1.03×, anything else to the owner"):

- **lower(I_j) > ln S → stop: PASS.**
- **upper(I_j) < ln K → stop: the gate's non-pass verdict** (FAIL / PARK / fallback / "to the owner", whichever the gate
  registered below K).
- Otherwise continue. **At the cap (the recorded N), the gate's own statistic and bands apply unchanged**: the ratio of
  cell means for a single-pass served gate, the geometric mean of the passes' ratios for a two-pass one, the paired
  median for an in-process gate. A gate's ambiguous band is reachable only at the cap.
- Inequalities are strict. The gate verdict combines the sub-gate verdicts by the gate's own rule (e.g. SHIP only if
  every graded model clears its bar). Models without a bar ("reported") have no stopping rule and run to the cap.
- **The recorded verdict** is what the pre-registered bands said, quoted from the record — not a later owner override.

### 1.4 Accounting, permutations and the decision

- **Runs used** = blocks consumed per paired arm (a served run, or an in-process pair), summed over a gate's models.
  Headline savings = 1 − Σ used / Σ recorded over every eligible gate, with reported-only models counted at their full
  recorded N (they would still run). The graded-models-only figure is printed beside it. Peer cells (Ollama,
  llama.cpp) are outside the paired A/B and are not counted (TE5(a) is their item).
- **Order sensitivity:** each gate is replayed over permutations of its blocks (a block keeps both arms' run *j*
  together, and its pairs stay inside their pass): every permutation if N! ≤ 200, otherwise 200 uniform random ones
  (seed 20260928). Reported, not graded; any FAIL → PASS in any order is called out as a finding.
- **Sensitivity arm, pre-registered with v1 and not the verdict:** V0 = the same rule with δ = 0 on served gates, to show
  what the allowance costs and buys.
- **Decision against TE4 (verbatim):** "It must reach the recorded verdict in ≥ 95% of gates, never flip a FAIL to a
  PASS, and use ≥ 25% fewer runs." Kill: "the replay disagrees on more than 5% of gates, flips any FAIL, or saves less
  than 25%." Agreement is judged per gate on the recorded order; a recorded non-pass (FAIL, PARK, ambiguous, "to the
  owner") replayed as PASS is counted as a flip of the disqualifying kind. **With fewer than 20 eligible gates a single
  disagreement already exceeds 5%**, so the agreement criterion is effectively "zero disagreements" and any verdict is
  provisional on the gate count.

### 1.5 Addendum, 2026-09-28 10:58 PDT (17:58 UTC), still before any replay

Written after the gate inventory (§2) was assembled and before `te4_replay.py` had replayed any gate. The rule in
§1.1–1.4 is unchanged. These are clarifications and one class extension.

1. **"The gate's own statistic" is taken from each record, not from §1.3's parenthesis.** §1.3's examples ("the ratio of
   cell means …") assumed a statistic, and the records differ. L1 gate 5 grades the ratio of the **medians** of the three
   runs (its table's 48.74 / 48.00 are run medians). The L1 arm64 fix and L1 gate 6 grade the ratio of `bench_peer.py`'s
   **rounded** cell `mean` field (111.5 / 107.1; `arm64-speed-combine.py` reads `["mean"]`). S6 grades the unrounded
   mean of runs. The peer gates grade per-pair labels over all run pairs (`peer-claim-2026-09-25/grade.py`). The
   script reproduces each and prints the recorded figure beside the recomputed one.
2. **A second in-process class: the CUDA q4k levers** (`q4k-lever1-2026-09-26/e2e.txt`,
   `q4k-lever3-2026-09-26/run{1-ungated,2-sizegated}/e2e.txt`). These are `BenchmarkResidentDecode` rounds: 6 paired
   rounds, arm order alternating by round, a fresh process per arm per round, the first sample of each process
   discarded (the record's medians and ranges are reproduced exactly by each process's second sample). Block = one
   round, d = ln(round ratio). **δ = 0**, as for the ABBA class, and for the same reason: every process-level effect is
   re-drawn in every block, so it is in SE, and the order alternates. This was decided before they were replayed.
3. **Peer gates** (`peer-claim-2026-09-25`, `q4k-peer-2026-09-26`) are replayed per graded (cell, peer) comparison with
   S = 1.03 and K = 0.97: an early PASS is the label AHEAD and an early FAIL is BEHIND. LEVEL and the AMBIGUOUS
   labels are reachable only at the cap, through the recorded grader. VOID comparisons are validity failures, not
   speed verdicts: they are not graded, and their cells run to the cap in the accounting.
4. **Accounting for shared cells.** In a peer gate one goinfer cell serves two comparisons (Ollama and llama.cpp).
   Runs are counted per engine cell. A cell runs until the last comparison it takes part in stops, so its count is
   the maximum over those comparisons. For a two-arm gate this equals 2 × blocks, the same as §1.4.
5. **S6 bands.** The measurement record's pre-registered band is used: pass ≤ 3% slower, 3–5% ambiguous / parked,
   > 5% fail (S = 0.97, K = 0.95). The task doc's wording ("within 3% … or it does not ship") has no separate fail
   bar.
6. **Flip direction for labels.** Rank AHEAD > {LEVEL, AMBIGUOUS-HIGH} > AMBIGUOUS-LOW > BEHIND, and PASS > AMBIG >
   FAIL. A disagreement is *toward pass* if the replay ranks higher than the record. It is **disqualifying** if the
   replay reaches the top verdict (PASS / AHEAD / the gate's ship decision) and the record did not.

|---|---|---|---|

**This is the finding that matters more than the 94.4%.** If Ollama's fast run had come last instead of first, the
rule would have stopped at run 2, called goinfer AHEAD of Ollama on this Mac's CPU, and never seen the run that
voids the claim. No interval built from the runs already seen can know about a run that has not happened. Only two
things prevent this:
- a prior on between-run variation, from the TE3 registry, that includes shifts of this size;
- a design in which such a shift hits both arms, as TE4's counterbalanced ABBA blocks do.

This replay cannot test that design: every served record here ran each arm as one uninterrupted cell.

## 6. The sensitivity arm and a post-hoc variant

**V0, δ = 0 on served gates** (pre-registered, not the verdict):
- the same 17 / 18 agreement and the same single disagreement;
- 0 disqualifying flips in recorded order;
- **36.9%** of cell-runs saved;
- over permuted orders, the same 2 disqualifying orders in peer-claim i.

So the δ = 0.022 allowance bought no safety on these gates: the one real failure is 14× larger than δ. It cost 7
points of savings, mostly on near-bar passes: S6 v14 saves 17% with it and 67% without.

**v1+ (designed after seeing v1's replay, on these same 18 gates — so it is not a validation, and is reported only to
size the fix).** It is v1 plus two conditions:
- served gates never stop before block 2;
- peer gates may not stop while either arm's run spread so far exceeds 5% (the record's own cap).

Results:
- in recorded order, 18 / 18 gates agree and there are 0 disqualifying flips;
- savings fall to **19.7%** (26.6% on graded cells), below the 25% bar;
- over permuted orders the **same 2 peer-claim i orders still flip to AHEAD at block 2**, because runs 2 and 3
  alone show no spread.

The cheap fixes buy agreement with savings, and do not close the order hole.

## 7. Verdict against TE4's acceptance criteria

| criterion (TE4, verbatim) | result | |
|---|---|---|
| "reach the recorded verdict in ≥ 95% of gates" | 17 / 18 = **94.4%** | **not met** |
| "never flip a FAIL to a PASS" | 0 in recorded order; **2 of 6 permuted orders of one gate flip AMBIGUOUS-LOW → AHEAD** | met as registered (recorded order); fails under reordering |
| "use ≥ 25% fewer runs" | **29.9%** of cell-runs (40.7% on graded cells) | met |
| **kill:** "disagrees on more than 5% of gates, flips any FAIL, or saves less than 25%" | 1 of 18 = 5.6% > 5% | **HIT** |

**TE4-SEQ-v1 is killed by its pre-registered replay.**
- **The margin is one gate of 18.** With fewer than 20 gates the verdict is provisional on the count, as §1.4 said.
  But it is not a technicality: the permutations show the same cell producing the disqualifying kind of error when
  the order changes.
- **The in-process classes are clean.** Six gates, every one of 960 orders agrees, 0 flips, and 24–27% of pairs and
  rounds saved in recorded order (31–36% on graded sub-gates). Each block there re-draws every process-level effect.
- **The served classes are where the risk and the savings both sit.** A sequential rule on served data needs two
  things this replay could not supply:
  - a between-run allowance from TE3's registry, sized to shifts like peer-claim i's 31%;
  - TE4's ABBA interleaving, so that a shift inside a pass lands on both arms.
- **Next step.** A TE4-SEQ-v2 has to be registered with both, and validated on gates **not** used here: the next
  nights' runs, or the ABBA-shaped served records excluded above once a reader exists. It should not be validated
  by re-replaying these 18.
