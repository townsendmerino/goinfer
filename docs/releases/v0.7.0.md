# goinfer v0.7.0

A pure-Go (no-cgo) LLM runtime. This release adds a **second vision-language family**,
**multi-tenant LoRA serving**, and the tools to **run models bigger than your RAM** —
all while the forward-pass numerics stay parity-gated against HuggingFace.

## Highlights

### 🖼️ Qwen2.5-VL — a second vision family (image → text, pure Go)
goinfer's vision support no longer stops at Gemma 3. Qwen2.5-VL works end-to-end —
HF-exact preprocessing (smart-resize + a faithful PIL-bicubic port + the spatial-merge
patchify), the pure-Go Qwen2.5-VL ViT + patch merger (native **dynamic resolution**),
and the part that made this hard: **m-RoPE** — Qwen's 3D (temporal/height/width)
rotary positions, ported and gated against HF for both prefill and decode. Point
`serve` at a Qwen2.5-VL checkpoint and send images over the OpenAI or Anthropic API.
Validated end-to-end on the real Qwen2.5-VL-3B. Text-only decode for every other model
is byte-for-byte unchanged.

### 🔌 Compute-time multi-adapter LoRA — N fine-tunes on one resident base
`serve --adapter <name>=<base>=<dir>` (repeatable). Instead of merging each LoRA into
its own full copy of the model, the low-rank `A`/`B` are applied in the forward
(`Y = W·x + s·B(A·x)`), so **N adapters of one base cost ≈ base + N small deltas**
instead of N × the whole transformer. Each adapter is a served model that shares the
base's resident weights but keeps its own KV sessions; route to one with the OpenAI
`model` field. Merged `--lora` remains the faster single-fine-tune default.

### 💾 Run a model bigger than your RAM
A suite of opt-in, default-off, bit-exact memory tools:
- **`--stream-weights`** pages weights out of an mmap'd `.giw` under a RAM budget
  (`--weight-cache <GB>`) — **MoE expert demand-paging** for sparse models and
  **dense per-layer streaming** for the rest. Run a 35B-A3B (or a dense model that
  won't fit) on far less RAM, trading cold-fault latency for footprint. Works on a
  plain `.gguf` too (transparent `.giw` sidecar cache).
- **`--quant int4mix`** — per-tensor mixed precision: attention + embed/head at int8,
  the int4-tolerant FFN bulk at int4. Near-int8 quality below int8 RAM, no new kernels,
  zero decode cost.
- **`--embed-int4`** — store the token-embedding/LM-head table at int4 (opt-in lossy;
  halves the largest tensor on big-vocab small models).
- **`--kv-idle-demote`** — tiered KV: snapshot idle warm sessions to NVMe and free
  their RAM, faulting them back byte-identically on the next request. Serve hundreds
  of intermittent chats on a small box.

## Upgrading
- **aikit → v1.8.1** (root + `gpu` modules) — brings the pure-Go Qwen2.5-VL vision
  tower.
- **darwin (Apple-silicon) now builds.** A `syscall.Madvise`-under-`//go:build unix`
  bug broke the macOS build; fixed per-platform. On darwin the `--weight-cache` cap is
  best-effort (the OS reclaims clean re-faultable pages under pressure) rather than
  self-enforced. CI now runs darwin/arm64 jobs.
- Tagged as both **`v0.7.0`** (runtime) and **`gpu/v0.7.0`** (the opt-in `-tags gpu`
  WebGPU backend).

## Validation
The forward-pass + quantization numerics are parity-gated against HuggingFace (the
stable contract). This release was swept green on **amd64 / Vulkan** (Linux, RTX 2070
SUPER) and **arm64 / Metal** (Apple silicon), including the Gemma 4 12B logit-parity
gate. The loader and architecture-descriptor surface remains pre-1.0 and may change as
new families and quant formats land.
