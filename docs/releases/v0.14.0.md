**The delta between the two commits is test-only**: `404b1af` env-gates two B13 *diagnostics*
behind `GOINFER_DIAG=1`. No production code differs between the phase-1 and phase-2 trees.

**Why phase 2 was re-run at all** (the honest version): on the first sweep those two diagnostics —
`locateDivergence` (710 s) and `routeFlipAtOutlier` (1727 s) — consumed ~41 minutes of phase 2's
120-minute budget and the run **timed out inside one of them**, which pushed
`TestQwen35GGUF_weightDiff`, a required 40-second gate, off the end of the run. The sweep reported
`1 BLOCKER · DID NOT RUN`, which was the correct call. After gating, `weightDiff` ran and passed
(41.71 s) and the two diagnostics report as honest SKIPs. See `R2` in `docs/queue-release.md`.

**The 2 SKIPs in phase 2 are those diagnostics, deliberately.** They are instruments that assert
almost nothing by design; they are not gates and must not sit in the sweep's timeout budget.

**Stamp half: NOT taken.** No `EMIT_MANIFEST=1` merge was run for this tag. The B15 emitter defect
that made the stamp untrustworthy is **fixed** (`6acdbfe`), so it is now available — but it was not
exercised here, and claiming a stamp that did not happen is the failure mode the fix exists to
prevent. The manifest is unchanged at its committed state.
